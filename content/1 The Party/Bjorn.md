[[Bjorn]] (played by Caiden) is a looming goliath fighter.

Often being a man of few words, he is always the first to wake up and is the last to go to sleep.

Considered by the party to be their ever-watchful eyes and ears, Bjorn is always on guard with his large two handed great sword (long sword?).

One of his eyes is slightly red, and the other is baby blue. There is a slight red tint in his veins as well.
