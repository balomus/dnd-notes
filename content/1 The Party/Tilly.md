[[Tilly]] (played by Stephen) is a dwarven bard who has developed a bit of a following here in [[Bryn Shander]] as the primary evening entertainer.

She is charming, a bit of a rogue, and always interested in an odd job, if the price is right.

[[Tilly]] was formerly an author of a book titled "The Hellbent Highborn", which accused several prominent political figures in Baldur's Gate and nobles in Waterdeep of being devil worshipers. Although the accusations were well founded, she made a lot of enemies and was forced to seek refuge in [[Icewind Dale]].
