# Cold Open
Our journey starts in [[Icewind Dale]], in a group of towns known as [[Ten-Towns]]. Specifically, the party finds themselves in the largest of the ten towns, a place known as [[Bryn Shander]].

The larger area of [[Ten-Towns]] has been experiencing a perpetual, dark winter for over 2 years. The sun no longer rises, and the harsh cold never ceases. Constant snowstorms, blizzards, and avalanches plague the entire area.

Most residents of [[Icewind Dale]] blame [[Auril, the Frost Maiden]], the god of winder's wrath. The shimmering aurora that weaves across the sky each night is said to be her doing, a potent spell that keeps the sun at bay.

Each of the ten towns has resolved to appease the Frostmaiden with sacrifices of one kind or another, but so far, the dark winter has been relentless.

# Notes
The party began by helping [[Recover a sled of iron ingots]] by slaying a few goblins, and carting the stolen sled back to [[Bryn Shander]].

[[Calypso]] was able to [[Find a family heirloom]], and did so without damaging [[Scramsax]]'s prized fish plaque, Ol' Bitey.

After getting some leads on the [[Recent Killings in Ten-Towns]], the party headed towards [[Caer-Konig]] where [[Sephek Kaltro]] was last seen.

On their way, they stopped briefly in [[Caer-Dineval]], where they ran into some cultists who appeared to know [[Tilly]]'s penname, Tildebrant. After trying to leave peacefully, they were forced to kill a few of the cultists before fleeing the town.

Shortly after, they arrived at [[Caer-Konig]] where they got some more details on [[Sephek Kaltro]]'s whereabouts.

They also learned about some [[Stolen Items in Caer-Konig]], and are helping the locals track them down with the assistance of [[Jarthra Farzassh]]. Following the tracks has lead them to a mountain outpost near [[Kelvin's Cairn]]...
