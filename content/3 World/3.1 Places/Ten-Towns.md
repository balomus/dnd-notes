[[Ten-Towns]] is a collection of ten individual towns that are loosely linked together via various roads and paths.

Every town has a speaker, a sort of mayor or representative figure.

With the relentless winter night that has lasted two years, the various towns have started making sacrifices to try and appease [[Auril, the Frost Maiden]]. The sacrifices take various shapes and sizes, with human sacrifices being the most heinous.
