[[Icewind Dale]] is the famously harsh, frozen tundra in the Forgotten Realms of Dungeons & Dragons.

Located far to the north, the large region is isolated by the Spine of the World, a large mountain range that prohibits travelers from easily coming and going.

The region is primarily known for the "[[Ten-Towns]]", a loose coalition of ten settlements.
