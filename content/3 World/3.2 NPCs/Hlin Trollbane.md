[[Hlin Trollbane]] (extra emphasis on the vocal fry) is a shield dwarf that the party ran into at [[The Northlook Inn and Tavern]].

She has taken a particular fondness of [[Calypso]], and believes her to be the party leader while refusing to work with [[Stacks]], who frequently annoys her by talking excessively and sharing what she deems to be "useless facts".

Hlin informed the party of some [[Recent Killings in Ten-Towns]].
