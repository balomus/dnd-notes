[[Hruna]], Korux, and Storn approached the party in [[The Northlook Inn and Tavern]] offering a small job for a handful of adventurers.

They are the survivors of a group of dwarves that were in the middle of delivering a sled filled with iron ingots to [[Bryn Shander]] when a yeti surprised their crew and killed several members of the group. These three are the only survivors.
# Reward
If the party can successfully retrieve the sled of iron ingots and bring it back safely to [[Bryn Shander]], [[Hruna]] will give each party member a gem worth 50gp.
# Party Progress
The party negotiated with the dwarves to give them their snowshoes to make traversing the winter landscape much easier.

The party was able to track down the sled, which was being stolen by a crew of 6 goblins and was about halfway between [[Bryn Shander]] and [[Kelvin's Cairn]]. After slaying the first few, the party tried to negotiate with the remaining goblins peacefully, but they were uninterested.

Before finishing off all of them, the goblins were able to alert their nearby caravan, where a leader figure appeared to watch the party after they finished off the last of the goblins and quickly left with the sleigh. The leader and caravan did not pursue the party.
# Conclusion
After getting back to [[Bryn Shander]] safely, the party received the promised reward, as well as a 10% discount at the [[Blackiron Blades]] on any future purchases.
