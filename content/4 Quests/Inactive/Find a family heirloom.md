[[Calypso]] felt a strong compulsion to check inside of Ol' Bitey, a mythical fish that is mounted on a plaque in [[The Northlook Inn and Tavern]].
# Conclusion
After some back and forth with [[Scramsax]], [[Calypso]] paid the agreed upon 50gp to search the fish.
# Reward
[[Calypso]] was able to reclaim her father's ring from inside the fish without damaging it.
