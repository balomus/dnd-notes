# Party Progress
The party purchased a dog sled from [[Frozenfar Expeditions]], as well some dogs to assist with pulling the sled. [[Jarthra Farzassh]] joined the party to assist in navigating the tundra as they continue to follow the tracks that lead out of town.

After investigating the tracks closely, they determined that a single Duergar scout jumped on a dogsled, which was then pulled away by an ogre. The tracks lead towards a mountain outpost near [[Kelvin's Cairn]].
