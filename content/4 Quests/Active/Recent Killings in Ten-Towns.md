[[Hlin Trollbane]] informed the party of some recent killings that have been happening across [[Ten-Towns]]. So far, there have been 3 murders in the past month:

1. A halfling trapper in Easthaven
2. A human shipbuilder in Targos
3. A dwarf glassblower in [[Bryn Shander]]

Each victim was found with a dagger of ice through the heart.

So far, there haven't been any promising leads. However, [[Hlin Trollbane]] believes that the culprit is [[Sephek Kaltro]], and she claims to know with 100% certainty that it is him. She says that she followed [[Torg's Traveling Merchant Company]] for a tenday, and noticed that [[Sephek Kaltro]] doesn't seem to be bothered by the cold whatsoever. He wears no coat, no scarf, no gloves. "Kiss of the Frostmaiden, indeed".
# Reward
If the party can track down [[Sephek Kaltro]], find proof that he is responsible for the murders, and bring his head to [[Hlin Trollbane]], she will reward them with 100 gp. She did indicate that the authorities should NOT be involved.
# Party Progress
The party got a tip that [[Sephek Kaltro]] is in [[Caer-Konig]] and headed that direction.

They have finally arrived, and upon speaking with some of the locals, it turns out [[Sephek Kaltro]] can be found at a storefront local to [[Caer-Konig]], where he is performing his bodyguard duties and watching over [[Torrga Icevein]].
